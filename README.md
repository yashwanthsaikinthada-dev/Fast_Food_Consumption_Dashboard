# 🍔 Fast Food Consumption & Digestive Health Dashboard – Power BI Project

---

## 📊 Overview
This project presents an interactive **Fast Food Consumption & Digestive Health Dashboard** developed in **Power BI**. The dashboard provides insights into digestive health issues, average fast food consumption, BMI, health scores, doctor visits, and physical activity across different age groups and genders. It enables health analysts to make data-driven decisions to promote healthier lifestyles.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Monitor prevalence of digestive issues among respondents
- Analyze average fast food meals consumed per week
- Examine correlations between BMI, health scores, and physical activity
- Compare trends by age group and gender
- Assess doctor visit frequency based on digestive health status

---

## 📊 Key Metrics Summary
- **Digestive Issue Prevalence:** 37.88%  
- **Average Fast Food Meals/Week:** 6.82  
- **Average BMI:** 26.44  
- **Average Health Score:** 4.92  

---

## 🔍 Dashboard Features
- **Filters:**  
  - Digestive Issues – Yes/No  
  - Gender – Female, Male, Other  
  - Age Group – <25, 25–40, 41–55, 55+  

- **Visualizations:**  
  - Pie chart: Count of age by digestive issues  
  - Bar chart: Average fast food meals per week by doctor visits and gender  
  - Line chart: Health score vs. physical activity hours per week  
  - Column chart: Doctor visits by digestive issues  

---

## 🧠 Insights & Observations
- Respondents with digestive issues consume slightly more fast food meals per week.  
- Males have a marginally higher average fast food intake than females.  
- Younger age groups (<25) consume more fast food but visit doctors less frequently.  
- Increased physical activity positively correlates with higher overall health scores.
